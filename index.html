The Mirror Warps

AI can simulate human perspectives.
But the reflection is not what you'd expect.

When researchers ask large language models to adopt a persona — a conservative from Georgia, a liberal from Portland — the AI produces convincing responses. But ask it the same question 50 times as the same persona, and something becomes clear:

Real humans disagree with each other. AI doesn't.

Below, you'll see this for yourself. Your AI responses will be compared to real survey data from the General Social Survey (GSS) 2022, one of the longest-running studies of American public opinion.

What you just witnessed is uniformity — one of nine systematic distortions identified in current AI models. Real people within the same demographic hold diverse, even contradictory opinions. The AI collapses that diversity into a narrow consensus that never existed.

The mirror reflects something, but it smooths away the texture of real human disagreement.

Based on "Simulating Subjects: The Promise and Peril of AI Stand-Ins for Social Agents and Interactions" by Austin C. Kozlowski and James A. Evans, University of Chicago.

Human data: General Social Survey 2022, NORC at the University of Chicago. AI model: Claude Haiku (Anthropic).
